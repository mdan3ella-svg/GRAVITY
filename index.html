Generalized eigenanalysis of the assembled 3D elastic frame with diagonal lumped translational and approximate rotary masses. Includes modal effective mass, SRSS/CQC combination, and a user-defined spectrum shape. Research use only; not code-certified.
Spectral acceleration equals Sa(g) on the plateau and decays as T₂/T thereafter; periods below T₁ transition linearly from 0.4 Sa to Sa. This is an illustrative user spectrum, not a prescribed NBCC/ASCE/Eurocode spectrum.

Product overview / design intent

Intelligence from the first structural line.

GRAVITY | Structure Defined is a standalone, browser-based conceptual structural BIM and analysis studio by VERTASCAN. It is designed around an intuitive idea: every modeled structural element carries geometry, section properties, mass, stiffness and loading information that can participate in an assembled structural system.

01 / MODEL

Design as connected structure

Draw line-to-beam geometry in 3D drafting planes or generate preconfigured portal, framed and braced structural assemblies. Configure nodes, supports, member sections, coordinate positions and material properties.

02 / COMPUTE

Explore building behavior

Estimate member and slab self-weight, add live and simplified lateral actions, solve a 3D elastic space frame, and inspect displacement, force response and comparative stress demand using connected analytics.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Multiple research workflows

Study preliminary second-order, material, buckling and cyclic behaviors in dedicated experimental tools. Compare simplified modal periods, participating mass and user-defined response-spectrum combinations.

04 / DOCUMENT

Traceable engineering review

Generate member quantities, analysis summaries, internal regression benchmarks, engineering review checklists, verification records and project files suitable for collaborative technical development.

Professional-use limitation: GRAVITY v9 is a research and concept-analysis prototype. Internal numerical checks do not constitute independent verification, code-certified structural design, licensed-engineer approval, or construction authorization. Detailed member and connection resistance checks and full nonlinear/seismic certification remain outside its validated scope.

Verification scope and development gates


Six-DOF elastic beam response and Newmark integration are experimental; bending/torsion are coupled linearly. The standalone fiber section integrates bilinear fibers through a rectangular cross-section and supports curvature reversal. Contact is rigid sphere versus member centerline approximation, not surface-to-surface finite-deformation contact. Full distributed nonlinear force-based beam sections, consistent corotational tangent, member-to-member impact coupling, geometric imperfections, seismic-code load combinations, and external peer-reviewed validation are still required.
